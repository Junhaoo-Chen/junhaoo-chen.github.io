Information processed

Notification delivery uses Bark. A Bark device key and APNs registration token are used to deliver notifications to an explicitly registered device. Notification content is processed only to deliver the requested message.

Use and retention

Information is used for authentication, authorization, service operation, security, and notification delivery. Credentials and device registrations are stored in restricted service configuration. No personal information is sold or used for advertising. Logs are retained only as needed for operation and security.

Third parties

Cloudflare provides DNS, secure proxying, Tunnel, and Access. Google provides identity authentication. Apple Push Notification service delivers Bark notifications. Each provider processes information under its own policies.

Your choices
