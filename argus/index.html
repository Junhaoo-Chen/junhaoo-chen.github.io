Argus Personal Assistant

Argus is a private, single-user assistant that helps its owner work with email, calendars, research documents, and other authorized services.

Argus is not offered as a public service and does not accept public account registrations. Access to Google data is granted directly by the owner through Google's OAuth consent flow.

How it is used

Argus retrieves and updates Google Workspace data only when needed to fulfill the owner's authenticated requests, such as finding a document, reviewing email, preparing a reply, or managing a calendar event. Sending email and other consequential actions remain subject to explicit confirmation.

Data handling

OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on a private server. Argus retains only limited operational state and does not sell personal information, use Google data for advertising, or make Google data available to unrelated parties.
