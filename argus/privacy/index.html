Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Argus is a private personal assistant operated solely for Junhao Chen. This policy explains how Argus handles Google user data authorized by its owner.

Data Argus accesses

With the owner's consent, Argus may access Google account profile information and authorized content from Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Slides. The specific access granted is shown in Google's OAuth consent screen.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to carry out the owner's authenticated requests. Examples include finding and summarizing messages or files, preparing email drafts, updating documents, and managing calendar events. Argus does not use Google user data for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated analytics.

AI processing

When necessary to fulfill a request, relevant content may be processed by the configured OpenAI Codex service for tasks such as summarization, classification, drafting, or analysis. Only content needed for the requested task is provided.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest on a private server with access restricted to the dedicated Argus service account. Argus stores limited operational metadata, confirmation records, schedules, and research state. External services remain the primary source of data; Argus does not intentionally create a complete local copy of the owner's Google account.

Sharing and disclosure

Argus does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Data is disclosed only to infrastructure and processing providers required to operate the requested functionality, or when legally required.

Retention and deletion

Temporary retrieved content is retained only as needed for the requested task. Minimal operational records and encrypted backups may be retained for reliability. The owner may revoke Argus through the Google Account permissions page and may request deletion of stored credentials and operational data by contacting the address below.

Google API Limited Use

Argus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to junhao1chen@gmail.com.
